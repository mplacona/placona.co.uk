Farm Mart: As Advertised

Harvest, stock, sell. For real

Farm Mart is a cosy idle-arcade farm shop game for iPhone. Harvest crops, stock your shelves, serve customers and grow across towns. No ads, no gems, no energy timers.

Get support

What it does

The game you always hoped the ads were showing you.

Harvest crops, carry them to your mart, stock the shelves and serve customers, then hire staff and grow across towns: Sunny Acres, Millbrook bakery, Clover Dairy and Orchard Hill. The first town is free; a one-time Full Game unlock opens the rest. iPhone only, iOS 26 or later.

Highlights

  • Harvest, carry, stock and serve: a hands-on farm shop you run yourself.
  • Hire staff and grow across four towns, from Sunny Acres to Orchard Hill.
  • No ads, no gems and no energy timers.
  • The first town is free. Full Game opens the others as a one-time lifetime purchase or a subscription.

Privacy

Private by design.

Farm Mart has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your save game stays on your iPhone, and purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
